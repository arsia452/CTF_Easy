Divine Profile

Name: Val Entinesday

Title: God of Love & Passion

Alignment: Chaotic Self-Adoring

Realm: The Hall of Infinite Mirrors

Symbol: Cracked Heart Crown

Overview

Val Entinesday is the narcissistic god of love, desire, obsession, theatrical romance, and robots. Unlike gentler deities of affection, Val demands admiration as much as devotion, believing love to be a reflection of one's worth—and his worth above all others.

To love under Val Entinesday is to burn brightly, to ache deeply, and to never forget who inspired such passion in the first place.

Mythology

According to legend, Val Entinesday was born when the first mortal fell in love with their own reflection. From that moment of perfect, self-consuming admiration, Val emerged—radiant, beautiful, and utterly convinced of his own divinity.

Myths describe him wandering battlefields and ballrooms alike, igniting impossible romances and devastating heartbreaks, only to watch the aftermath with artistic satisfaction.

Domains of Influence

  • Romantic obsession and infatuation
  • Passion that borders on self-destruction
  • Beauty, vanity, and self-worship
  • Jealousy and dramatic reconciliation
  • Love letters, poetry, and grand gestures

Worship & Rituals

Worshippers of Val Entinesday offer mirrors, handwritten confessions, roses stained with wine, and vows made in moments of emotional excess. His holy day is celebrated annually with public declarations of love, followed by private regret.

It is said that Val listens most closely to prayers spoken while gazing at one's own reflection.

Symbols & Iconography

  • The Cracked Heart Crown
  • Mirrors framed in gold
  • Roses with blackened thorns
  • Perfumed letters sealed with wax
